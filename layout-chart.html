Rules

  • A consonant key alone gives the bare letter: a → க
  • The next vowel key becomes its sign: a x → கி
  • அ after a consonant gives the pulli: a z → க்
  • Caps Lock makes the vowel long: ^z → ஆ, q ^z → வா
  • Shift gives the grantha letters: ஸ ஷ ஜ ஹ க்ஷ, and ⇧z is a lone ்
  • Space, digits, punctuation and Ctrl/Alt shortcuts pass straight through
